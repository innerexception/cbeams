Factions start with a pre-set number of logistics centers from which units may be spawned. 
Defenses may be deployed within a radius of a logistics center.
Total logistics available = number of logistics centers * 10
Logistics centers must be placed at least 500px away from another logistics center


Placement phase
Place logi centers

Buildings have point value
Place all other buildings up to your starting point value
Buildings must be placed near a logi center

Objective areas spawn at random in the middle of the map. Control all objectives for 1 min to win.
Control can only be achieved using ARMOR or IFV
