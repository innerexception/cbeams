Each mission starts with a preset fleet.
Individual units in the fleet may be veterans from a previous battle
Veterancy increases stats and may grant a unique ability
Building a new non-drone ship requires an AI core, which is lost on ship destruction
New cores are gained when capturing objectives

Heretic vessels:
HUSK - hi hp low damage fodder
STITCHER - creates HUSKS / KKZ
BEHOLDER - beam frigate, long range slow firing. retreats to max range.
BLADE - fast, short range damage flanker. Can fire large dumb-fire missile.
BOM - large fusion warhead on a stick

Orthodox vessels:
SPR - long range missile frigate
PDF - point defense frigate
STL - med range gunboat

Both:
GAIN - resupply
ZEL - captures objectives / infiltrates enemy ships

Missions:

1. Ambush
    The heretics have opened a portal deep inside our space. It appears they want the keys.
    Destroy the enemy with the small force at your disposal to prevent them from desecrating our holy sites.
        Objective: Do not allow any objective to be captured. Let none survive.

    -HUSKS emerge from the nebula at the top right and attempt to escort the three enemy ZELs to the artifact sites.
